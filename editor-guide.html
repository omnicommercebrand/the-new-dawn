How to Edit Your Website

A simple guide to changing words, swapping photos, choosing colors, and reordering things — no tech skills needed. You cannot break anything. Visitors only ever see what you choose to Publish, and you can always undo with History.

1 Getting In

  1. Go to anewdawn.earth/editor
  2. Type your editor password and click Sign in

Your password: Ishan sent this to you privately on WhatsApp. Keep it somewhere safe and private — anyone with it can edit your site.

2 The Lay of the Land

When you sign in you'll see two halves:

  • Left side — your editing tools (the panels)
  • Right side — a live preview of your site that updates as you type

Along the top:

ButtonWhat it does
? HelpOpens a quick reminder of everything below
View LiveOpens your real, published site in a new tab
Save DraftSaves your work privately (visitors don't see it yet)
PublishMakes your changes go live for everyone
LogoutSigns you out

The tabs — Content · Theme · Arrange · Images · History — are the five things you can change. We'll go through each one.

Draft vs. Live (important): Everything you do here is a private draft. Your visitors keep seeing the old version until you click Publish. So feel free to experiment — nothing is public until you say so.

3 Changing Words (Content tab)

This is where you edit any text on the page.

Two easy ways to edit

  • Click the text right in the preview (right side). The editor jumps straight to that text box for you.
  • Or scroll the Content panel on the left and find the text box you want.

To make text bold or italic — no codes to type

  1. Select the words you want to change (drag over them, or double-click a word)
  2. Click B for bold or I for italic in the little toolbar above the box (Shortcut: Ctrl/Cmd + B for bold, Ctrl/Cmd + I for italic)
  3. ↵ adds a line break · Clear format removes all bold/italic

As you type, the preview on the right updates instantly.

See a small reset link next to a text box? That means you've changed it. Click reset to put that one piece of text back to how it was.

4 Pictures (Images tab)

Each editable image has its own box. To change one:

  • Have the photo on your computer? Click Upload and choose it. (Up to 15 MB · JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF.)
  • Have a web link to the photo? Paste the link (it must start with https://) into the box.

The preview updates right away. Made a mistake? Click Reset on that image to bring back the original.

Tip: large photos make your site slower. Square or landscape photos around 1500–2500 pixels wide look great and load fast.

5 Colors & Theme (Theme tab)

  • Pick a palette: Choose one of the ready-made color sets and your whole site re-colors instantly. This is the easy, safe way to change the mood.
  • Fine-tune a single color: Below the palettes you can nudge individual colors (like Accent gold or Main text) if you want. Each one has a reset to undo just that color.

Try a few — the preview shows it live, and nothing is public until you Publish.

6 Reorder & Hide (Arrange tab)

  • Reorder: Use the ↑ / ↓ arrows to move items (like the tags under your headline) up or down.
  • Show / Hide: Flip a switch off to hide a whole section from your site, or on to show it again. Hiding never deletes anything — flip it back anytime.

7 Saving & Publishing

There's a difference, and it matters:

  • Save Draft — tucks your work away safely so you can come back later. Visitors still see the old version.
  • Publish — pushes everything live for the world to see.

A small dot appears when you have unsaved changes. If you try to leave with unsaved work, the browser will warn you.

Normal rhythm: edit → glance at the preview → Publish when you're happy.

8 Undo Anything (History tab)

Every time you Publish, we keep a snapshot. If something doesn't look right:

  1. Open the History tab
  2. Find an earlier version by its date and time
  3. Click Restore — it loads that version back as your draft to review
  4. Happy with it? Click Publish

So you can always roll back to a good version. Nothing is ever truly lost.

9 On Your Phone

The editor works on your phone too. Because the screen is smaller, you'll see an Edit / Preview switch:

  • Edit — shows the editing tools
  • Preview — shows your site

Tapping a piece of text in Preview jumps you to Edit for that text. Everything else works the same.

Quick Answers

  • Will I break the site? No. Drafts are private, and History lets you undo.
  • I changed something by accident. Click reset next to that item, or use History to restore an earlier version.
  • My change isn't showing on the real site. You probably saved a draft but didn't Publish. Click Publish.
  • I forgot my password. Message Ishan on WhatsApp and he'll send it to you again.

One move at a time. Edit, preview, publish. You've got this. 🌅
