How to edit your site

Welcome! This is where you change the words, pictures, colors, and order of things on your website. Nothing you do here is public until you press Publish.

The two safety zones

  • Draft = your private workspace. Only you see it.
  • Live = what visitors see. It only updates when you click Publish.

Editing text

  1. Pick a page from the dropdown at the top.
  2. Click any text right in the preview, or find it under the Content tab.
  3. Type your new wording. To make a word bold or italic, highlight it and press the B or I button above the box.
  4. Watch it change instantly in the preview.

Pictures

Open the Images tab. Either Upload a photo from your computer, or paste an image link. Press Reset to go back to the original.

Colors & theme

Open the Theme tab. Tap one of the 6 ready-made palettes to restyle the whole site. Want to tweak one color? Use Fine-tune Colors below.

Reorder & hide

Open the Arrange tab to move sections up/down or switch parts of the page on and off.

Saving your work

  1. Save Draft (or press Ctrl/Cmd + S) keeps your progress privately.
  2. Publish makes it live for everyone.
  3. History tab lets you roll back to any earlier published version.

A small dot next to the page name means you have unsaved changes. The reset link on any field puts it back to the original.
